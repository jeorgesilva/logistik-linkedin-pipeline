# 04 — Topic Patterns
*Rules for specific content categories — how tone, structure, and framing shift by topic*

---

## Topic 1: Industry Crisis / Negative News
*(insolvency wave, freight capacity collapse, carrier failures)*

**Posicionamento aplicável (`06_point_of_view.md`):** crença 4 (preço baixo = sintoma de queima de substância) e crença 7 (crise estrutural, atinge compradores com 6–12 meses de atraso). Um post de opinião sobre este tópico normalmente ancora em uma das duas, ou nas duas combinadas — Pattern F é uma opção tão válida quanto Pattern C/E aqui.

### Framing principles
- **Do not editorialize** on the failed companies. Name them, state the facts, move on.
- **Shift blame to the system**, not individuals: *„nicht weil sie schlechte Unternehmen waren — weil das System um sie herum aufgehört hat zu funktionieren"*
- **Time-delay framing** is essential: the crisis reaches Verlader with a 6–12 month lag. This makes the post actionable for readers who don't feel affected yet.
- **Never use** "leider", "tragisch", "bedauerlicherweise" — these are too soft and signal editorial rather than analysis.

### Structure
1. Hard number as headline
2. Named company examples (if available) — adds human scale
3. Systemic cause explanation (not individual failure)
4. The delayed-impact warning (who should care and why now)
5. Early warning signals (3 bullets, `→` arrows)
6. What type of action the situation calls for (category-level, not product pitch)
7. LogAid connection (brief, logical)
8. CTA to blog if deep analysis exists

### Tone markers
- Calm authority. Not panic.
- Sentences get shorter as urgency builds.
- One memorable aphorism at the end.

### Example closing constructions
- *„Ein Signal ist kein Drama. Mehrere gleichzeitig sind ein Alarm."*
- *„Günstige Preise sind oft das Resultat eines Dienstleisters, der seine Substanz verbrennt."*
- *„Wenn der kippt, kippt Ihre Lieferkette. Ohne Vorwarnung."*

## Topic 2: Market Trend / Industry Data Report
*(Microsoft AI report, DVZ analysis, Eurostat freight data)*

**Posicionamento aplicável (`06_point_of_view.md`):** crença 1 (comunicação é o custo invisível, o setor olha para o que brilha) e crença 8 (adoção de IA é mais hype do que prática — barreira técnica caiu, falta integração). A maioria dos relatórios de mercado serve para provar uma dessas duas teses, não para ser noticiada neutramente.

### Framing principles
- **Source first**: name the report, date, publisher — within the first 2 sentences.
- **The insight must be reframed** for German Mittelstand logistics specifically. Global data means nothing until it's translated: *„Asien zieht massiv an, sobald die Sprachmodelle in der Landessprache funktionieren. Für deutschsprachige Speditionen heißt das..."*
- **Pick 3–5 data points** and format them with emojis as visual anchors.
- **Contrast**: the global trend versus the German Dispo reality.

### Structure
1. Hook stat (bold, surprising)
2. Source attribution (report name, date, publisher)
3. 3–5 bullet data points with emoji
4. The "what this means for Logistik specifically" paragraph
5. LogAid tie-in (positioned as the operational answer to the trend)
6. Link to original report (credibility)

### Tone markers
- Analytical but not dry — always return to the Disponent's daily life.
- Emojis only for data bullets in this format, not elsewhere.
- Ending can include a mild warning: *„Wer jetzt nicht in produktive KI-Anwendungen geht, fällt nicht morgen zurück — sondern hat es bereits."*

## Topic 3: Regulatory / Political Event
*(AI model shutdown, DSGVO enforcement, US tech policy affecting German businesses)*

**Posicionamento aplicável (`06_point_of_view.md`):** crença 5 (soberania de dados é gestão de risco, não bandeira política). Este é o tópico onde a linha entre opinião e posição política é mais fina — a crença 5 existe justamente para manter o argumento no terreno operacional.

### Framing principles
- **Open with the specific event** — date, what happened, who acted. No opinion yet.
- **The pivot sentence** is the most important: *„Für die meisten ist das eine Tech-Schlagzeile. Für eine Spedition ist es etwas anderes."*
- **What's at stake for the Spedition** — name the specific data types at risk (Sendungsdaten, Preise, Empfängeradressen, Schadensfälle).
- **No partisan framing.** The post is not anti-US or pro-EU. It's pro-operational-control.
- **Datenhoheit** as logical conclusion, not political position.

### Structure
1. Event (factual, specific)
2. Most people read this as X. For a Spedition, Y.
3. What exactly is exposed / at risk
4. LogAid's architectural response to this risk
5. Closing aphorism — philosophical but grounded
6. Optional: link to blog for deeper analysis

### Tone markers
- Most restrained tone of all categories. Let the logic do the work.
- Avoid adjectives. Let nouns and verbs carry the argument.
- The LogAid mention focuses on architecture, not features.

## Topic 4: Product / Capability Showcase
*(What LogAid actually does, specific automation workflows)*

**Posicionamento aplicável (`06_point_of_view.md`):** crença 2 (sugestão não é automação, é mais um clique) e crença 3 (sistemas de registro documentam, não agem — quem decide é o postfach) e crença 6 (IA genérica entende linguagem, não spedition). Este tópico é o único onde o produto é o protagonista por definição — mas ainda deve ser ancorado numa dessas crenças, não apenas descrito.

### Framing principles
- **Show the specific workflow**, not the category: *„Statusabfragen, ETA-Updates, Rückfragen aus dem Postfach, Stammdatenpflege"* — not *„administrative tasks"*.
- **Quantify the problem first**: *„Hunderte von E-Mails täglich. Manuell. Kopiert von Disponenten, die bereits die meist belasteten Menschen im Gebäude sind."*
- **The hero is the Disponent getting time back**, not LogAid's technology.
- Integration story matters: *„Über TMS, Telematik und Postfach hinweg. Keine Migration. Kein Umlernen."*

### Structure
1. The daily pain (concrete, operational)
2. What it costs (time, error rate, Disponent burnout — implied)
3. What changes (specific workflows, not platform-level)
4. Integration without disruption
5. Who benefits (Disponent, Unternehmer)
6. CTA to demo or blog

### Tone markers
- Confident. Zero hedging.
- Short active sentences.
- *„Nur die Stunden zurück, die jeden Tag verloren gehen"* — the emotional anchor is time, not technology.

## Topic 5: Aviation / Infrastructure Logistics
*(Helicopter operations, power line construction, remote area projects)*

**Posicionamento aplicável (`06_point_of_view.md`):** nenhuma, por design. Este tópico constrói credibilidade operacional, não argumenta uma tese — não force uma crença aqui.

### Framing principles
- **Dramatic contrast** as opener: *„Wenn konventionelle Methoden an ihre Grenzen stoßen... fliegen wir weiter."*
- **Three-quality format** with emojis (🛠️ Effizient / 🛡️ Sicher / 🌱 Minimalinvasiv) — proven structure.
- **Bilingual** — always provide both DE and EN blocks, full separator between them.
- **Environment and precision** are always mentioned. Not optional.
- These posts build credibility for operational excellence even beyond the AI product context.

### Structure
1. Dramatic opener (the scenario at its limits)
2. The need for precision (what's at stake)
3. What makes this approach different (one sentence)
4. Three-quality bullet block
5. Hashtags — both languages
