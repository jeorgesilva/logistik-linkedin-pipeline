## German Logistics Glossary
*(For correct terminology in posts and RAG context)*

### Operations & Dispatch

| Term | Definition | English equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| **Disposition / Dispo** | The planning and coordination of freight transport; the core function LogAid automates | Dispatch / Dispatching |
| **Disponent** | The person responsible for dispatch; primary LogAid user | Dispatcher |
| **Freileitungsbau** | Construction of overhead power lines | Overhead power line construction |
| **Netzausbau** | Expansion of the power grid or transport network | Grid/network expansion |
| **Avisierung** | Pre-notification of a delivery to the recipient | Delivery pre-notification / Advising |
| **ETA** | Estimated Time of Arrival — used as-is in German logistics | ETA |
| **Sendungsdaten** | Shipment data (tracking, weight, route) | Shipment data |
| **Stammdatenpflege** | Maintenance of master data (addresses, carrier profiles) | Master data maintenance |
| **Statusabfrage** | Status inquiry — e.g. "where is the truck?" | Status query |
| **Eskalation** | Escalation of a delayed or failed shipment | Escalation |
| **TMS** | Transport Management System | TMS |
| **Telematik** | Telematics — GPS/vehicle tracking systems | Telematics |
| **Frachtführer** | Carrier (the company that physically moves the freight) | Carrier |
| **Verlader** | Shipper (the company that initiates the freight) | Shipper / Consignor |

### Business & Market

| Term | Definition | English equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| **Mauterhöhung** | Toll increase (a key cost driver in German road freight) | Toll increase |
| **Insolvenz** | Insolvency / bankruptcy | Insolvency |
| **Substanz verbrennen** | Burning through company reserves to stay competitive (unsustainable) | Burning reserves |
| **Zahlungsziele** | Payment terms (e.g. net 90 days) — a major cash flow issue for small carriers | Payment terms |
| **Kapazitätsengpass** | Capacity shortage | Capacity crunch |
| **Liquiditätskrise** | Liquidity crisis | Liquidity crisis |
| **Mittelstand** | SME segment — the backbone of German logistics; roughly €1M–€50M revenue | SME / Mid-market |
| **Vorauszahlung** | Advance payment (a distress signal when carriers start asking for it) | Advance payment / Prepayment |
| **Betriebssicherheit** | Operational security / business continuity | Operational security |
| **Nachfolge / Nachfolger** | Business succession — a structural challenge in German Mittelstand logistics | Business succession |

### Regulation & Policy

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **DSGVO** | Datenschutz-Grundverordnung — EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) |
| **Datenhoheit** | Data sovereignty — who controls where and how data is stored and processed |
| **behördliche Anordnung** | Regulatory order / government directive |
