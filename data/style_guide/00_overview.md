## The One-Sentence Brand Voice

> **Wir sprechen wie ein Praktiker, der die Zahlen kennt — und keine Zeit hat für Selbstbeweihräucherung.**  
> *(We speak like a practitioner who knows the numbers — and has no patience for self-congratulation.)*

---

## Core Identity Signals (must appear implicitly in every post)

1. **Data sovereignty** — DSGVO-konform, Verarbeitung in Europa, keine Abhängigkeit von US-Clouds
2. **Operational urgency** — The crisis is now. Not in 12 months.
3. **Practitioner credibility** — We know what a Disponent's inbox looks like at 07:30 on a Monday
4. **No-hand-holding AI** — Automation that runs inside existing workflows (TMS, Telematik, Postfach), no migration theater

---

## Language Split Policy

| Language | When to use |
|---|---|
| **Deutsch (primary)** | Posts targeting DACH market operators, crisis/regulation/insolvency news, product posts |
| **English** | Posts on global industry data, international logistics trends, employer branding |
| **Bilingual (DE + EN)** | Technical capability showcases, posts with international reach (e.g. helicopter/infrastructure) |

**Rule:** Never mix languages mid-paragraph. Full block in DE, then separator `‐-------`, then full block in EN.
