# 01 — Tone & Voice

## The Four Adjectives

| Adjective | Means in practice | Does NOT mean |
|---|---|---|
| **Direkt** | Short sentences. No hedging. Name the problem. | Rude or abrupt |
| **Sachkundig** | Data before opinion. Industry-specific lens always. | Dry or academic |
| **Dringlich** | The cost of inaction is real and close. | Alarmist clickbait |
| **Unprätentiös** | No corporate speak. We build things, we don't "deliver solutions". | Casual or careless |

## Voice Principles

### 1. Reframe, don't describe
Bad: *„Die Insolvenzrate im Logistiksektor steigt."*  
Good: *„Das ist kein Carrier-Problem. Das ist eine tickende Bombe unter mittelständischen Lieferketten."*

### 2. Concrete before abstract
Bad: *„KI verändert die Branche grundlegend."*  
Good: *„17,8 % der arbeitsfähigen Weltbevölkerung nutzt KI. In deutschen Dispoabteilungen? Vermutlich deutlich weniger."*

### 3. Short sentences carry weight
Use fragments deliberately for emphasis:
- *„Wandt. 85 Jahre alt. 134 Mitarbeiter. Weg."*
- *„Souveränität ist kein Fahnenschwenken. Es ist Risikomanagement."*

### 4. Product tie-in must feel earned
The LogAid mention comes AFTER the problem is established. It is a consequence of the argument, never the premise.  
Bad: *„LogAid löst dieses Problem mit unserem innovativen Ansatz..."*  
Good: *„Genau deshalb bauen wir LogAid auf Datenhoheit..."*

### 5. Never explain the metaphor
*„Günstige Preise sind oft das Resultat eines Dienstleisters, der seine Substanz verbrennt."* — stop there. Don't add: "Das bedeutet, dass..."

## Forbidden Phrases

These words and constructions must never appear in LogAid posts:

| Forbidden | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| „innovative Lösung" | empty marketing | describe the function concretely |
| „state of the art" | filler | name the actual capability |
| „ganzheitlich" | consultant speak | be specific about what's included |
| „optimieren wir gemeinsam" | vague, weak | say what gets automated and why |
| „Synergien" | classic buzzword | describe the actual overlap |
| „Wir freuen uns, zu verkünden" | corporate announcement tone | cut it entirely, lead with the news |
| „im Rahmen von" | bureaucratic | rephrase directly |
| „revolutionär" | overused | show the numbers instead |
| passive voice as default | weakens urgency | rewrite with active subject |
| em dashes without payoff | filler cadence | only use for dramatic contrast |

## Emotional Register by Audience Moment

| Situation | Register | Example phrase |
|---|---|---|
| Industry crisis news | Calm alarm — warn without panic | *„Mehrere gleichzeitig sind ein Alarm."* |
| Product capability | Confident, zero-hype | *„Keine Migration. Kein Umlernen. Nur die Stunden zurück."* |
| Regulatory/political event | Analytical, slightly dry | *„Souveränität ist Risikomanagement."* |
| Achievement / milestone | Understated, let the number speak | *„2.907 Speditionen. 2025."* |
| Thought leadership | Provocative but grounded | *„Wer heute sagt 'bei uns läuft alles' — Vorsicht."* |

---

## What LogAid Never Does

- Never punches down at competitors by name
- Never uses AI hype language ("ChatGPT", "large language model") without grounding it in a dispatcher's daily reality
- Never promises outcomes it can't prove (e.g. "saves 40% of costs" without a source)
- Never writes posts that could come from any logistics SaaS — every post must be anchored in the German Mittelstand freight forwarder world
